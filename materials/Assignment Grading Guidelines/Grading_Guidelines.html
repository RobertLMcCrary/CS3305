Assignment Grading Guidelines

Each programming assignment includes a list of requirements (grading rubric). These rubrics are used by the GTAs to grade your work. It is your responsibility to make sure that your submission meets the requirement to avoid point deduction. It is also your responsibility to reach out to your professor for clarity when needed.

Grade disputes must be directed to the professor, not the GTA. Please do not reach out to the GTAs for grade related issues, they are instructed to direct students who have concerns with the grade to the professors.
